Quick Describe 0.1.4 can describe what's in front of your PC's camera, and can keep a copy of every picture it describes.

## Install

If you have Quick Describe already, it updates itself: Windows checks every few hours and installs the new version while Quick Describe isn't running, and **Help, Check for Updates** checks now.

To install it for the first time, download and open **QuickDescribe.appinstaller**. The other files on this release are what the installer downloads; you don't need to open them yourself.

**You need** a Copilot+ PC with a recent version of Windows 11 for descriptions. On other PCs, Quick Describe reads the text in pictures but doesn't describe them.

## What's changed

- **Describe Camera.** **Describe, Camera** (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M from any app, or the new Describe Camera button) takes a picture with your PC's camera and describes it, with the text in it. Quick Describe comes to the front and says "Taking a picture with the camera". It uses the camera on the back of a tablet if there is one, then the one facing you, then one you've plugged in. The first time, Windows asks whether Quick Describe may use the camera. If another app such as the Camera app is showing the camera, Quick Describe shares it. If you'd changed the hotkeys from Ctrl+Alt+Shift with a letter, or already given M to another command, the camera has no hotkey until you choose one in Settings.
- **Save Described Image and Copy Described Image.** Save Screenshot and Copy Screenshot have new names, since they work on whatever was just described: the screen, a window, the focused item, the camera's picture, and now a clipboard picture or a picture file too. The keys are the same, Ctrl+S and Ctrl+Shift+S.
- **Save every picture.** A new choice in **File, Settings**, off unless you turn it on: **Save a copy of every picture Quick Describe describes** keeps each one in a Quick Describe folder in your Pictures folder, named with what it was and when: camera pictures as JPEG, the rest as PNG. Picture files you open aren't copied. If your Pictures folder is backed up to OneDrive, these copies are uploaded with it.
