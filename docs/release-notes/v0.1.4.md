Quick Describe 0.1.4 can describe what's in front of your PC's camera.

## Install

If you have Quick Describe already, it updates itself: Windows checks every few hours and installs the new version while Quick Describe isn't running, and **Help, Check for Updates** checks now.

To install it for the first time, download and open **QuickDescribe.appinstaller**. The other files on this release are what the installer downloads; you don't need to open them yourself.

**You need** a Copilot+ PC with a recent version of Windows 11 for descriptions. On other PCs, Quick Describe reads the text in pictures but doesn't describe them.

## What's changed

- **Describe Camera.** **Describe, Camera** (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M from any app, or the new Describe Camera button) takes a picture with your PC's camera and describes it, with the text in it. Quick Describe comes to the front and says "Taking a picture with the camera"; the camera's light is on for a second or two while it adjusts, and goes off once the picture is taken. It uses the camera on the back of a tablet if there is one, then the one facing you, then one you've plugged in. The first time, Windows asks whether Quick Describe may use the camera. Save Screenshot and Copy Screenshot work on the camera's picture too. If you'd changed the hotkeys from Ctrl+Alt with a letter, or already given M to another command, the camera has no hotkey until you choose one in Settings.
